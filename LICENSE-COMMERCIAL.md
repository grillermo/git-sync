# Commercial licensing

git-sync is free software under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**
(see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). That is the only licence it ships under, and for
most people it is the only one they will ever need.

## You do not need to read any further if

- you use git-sync on your own machines, at home or at work;
- you fork it, modify it, and keep those changes to yourself;
- you publish your fork or your patches under the AGPL as well.

All of that is what the AGPL is for, and none of it costs anything.

## When the AGPL starts to matter

The AGPL is copyleft, and section 13 extends that to use over a network. So if
you want to:

- ship git-sync, or anything derived from it, as part of a product you
  distribute;
- offer it to third parties as a hosted or managed service;
- embed it in a closed-source system;

then everything you combine it with has to be released under the AGPL too.

If that is not something your company can do, the AGPL is not your only option:
a separate commercial licence is available, which grants the same rights without
the copyleft obligation.

I hold the copyright on all of git-sync, so I can licence it to you on other
terms. Open an issue at
<https://github.com/grillermo/git-sync/issues> titled "commercial licence", or
write to the address on my GitHub profile, and tell me roughly what you want to
build. Terms are negotiated per case; there is no price list.

## Contributing

Patches are welcome under the AGPL. Note that, because dual licensing requires
a single copyright holder, substantial contributions may need a contributor
licence agreement before they can be merged — ask in the pull request and we
will sort it out there.
